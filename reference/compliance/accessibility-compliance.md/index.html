# Accessibility compliance WSO2 Integrator: MI conforms with Level A and Level AA success criteria and techniques of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 (WCAG 2.1). This makes content on WSO2 Integrator: MI more accessible to a broader range of people with disabilities and makes the product more usable in general. WSO2 provides a [Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT) document]({{base_path}}/assets/attachments/reference/VPAT_wso2_MI.pdf) for WSO2 Integrator: MI with details of the product's compliance with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 (WCAG 2.1).
