# Google Ads Connector Overview Google Ads are customizable advertising units that allow businesses to reach their target audience on Google and its associated platforms. These ads can be tailored with various formats, targeting options, and budget settings. The Google Ads Connector allows you to access the Google Ads API. This lets you create, update, and delete ad campaigns and ads.
