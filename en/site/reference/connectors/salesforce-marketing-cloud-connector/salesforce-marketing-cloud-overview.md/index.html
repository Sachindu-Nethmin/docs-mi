# Salesforce Marketing Cloud Connector Overview Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) Connector empowers businesses to engage their target audience with personalized, multi-channel marketing communications. With Salesforce Marketing Cloud, you can design and execute dynamic email campaigns, SMS messages and more. The Salesforce Marketing Cloud Connector offers seamless access to the Salesforce Marketing Cloud Engagement REST API, enabling you to create, update, and manage comprehensive marketing campaigns and customer journeys with ease.
